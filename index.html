About Program 300

Program 300 is presented as a resource coordination and human development platform for participating members. Its stated purpose is to help members identify resources, organize personal goals, track referrals, and pursue long-term stability through responsible self-development.

New World Order Private Government DAO LLC is an independent organization. It is not an Indiana or federal government agency.
